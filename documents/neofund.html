What is this
The Problem
Running a cross-border fund requires $50–200K in legal setup, a licensed administrator, prime broker relationships, and ongoing compliance overhead. Only institutional managers can access this infrastructure today.
The Solution
A self-serve platform where sophisticated individuals can form, raise for, and operate a legal fund in days — not months. Cardano smart contracts replace the administrative overhead. Alpaca replaces the prime broker relationship.
The Model
Platform is a technology and infrastructure provider — not an adviser or broker. GPs are legally responsible for their fund. LPs interact only with the GP's whitelabeled app. Smart contracts enforce the declared thesis — no discretionary drift.
Actors
LP — Investor
Limited Partner
Accredited or sophisticated. Subscribes in USDC or ADA via the GP's branded app. Never interacts with the platform directly. Receives pro-rata distribution when the fund's thesis exits.
GP — Manager
Fund Operator
Passes platform KYC and sophistication gate. Declares a fixed thesis at formation — tickers, position limits, hold period. Executes on Alpaca within those parameters. Legally responsible for LP compliance and fund conduct.
Platform
Infrastructure Layer
Provides formation docs, smart contract deployment, Alpaca account configuration, portfolio reporting, and distribution rails. Holds Alpaca API keys per fund to enforce thesis constraints. Never holds LP funds directly.
Smart Contract Components — Capital Flow
Capital Flow Architecture
Money enters as USDC or ADA, is held and routed by smart contracts, executes as equities on Alpaca, then returns and splits programmatically. Platform never holds a balance — contracts are the counterparty at every step.

Cardano Template Mapping — MVP Only
Contract Cardano Template Role in Neo-Fund
Factory factory Deploys a fresh contract set per fund when a GP creates a new fund. One transaction — GP gets their own Escrow, Fund Contract, Config, and Splitter. This is what makes it self-serve.
Escrow escrow / crowdfund Fundraise safety net only. Holds LP subscriptions until the raise target and deadline are both met. If the fund doesn't form — target missed or GP cancels — it unwinds and refunds every LP automatically. No other role.
Fund Contract escrow (spend validator) GP-controlled address with a redeemer lock. GP can withdraw funds — but the validator only allows spending to one whitelisted destination: their designated Alpaca account address. Prevents capital going anywhere else. Platform is not the custodian; the constraint is the custodian.
Config storage Stores the declared thesis on-chain at formation: allowed tickers, position limits, hold period. Immutable once the raise closes. LPs can read it at any time. Basis for legal recourse if GP deviates.
Splitter payment splitter Receives proceeds when GP sends them back from Alpaca. Executes the waterfall: return of capital pro-rata to LP wallets → carry to GP → platform fee. Reads LP shares from the Registry.
Registry simple wallet / storage Records each LP's wallet address and contribution at subscription time. Source of truth for the Splitter's pro-rata calculation. Written at raise close, read-only after.
